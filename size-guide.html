HOW TO MEASURE YOUR FOOT LENGTH AT HOME

  1. Place a blank sheet of paper flat on the floor against a wall.
  2. Stand on the paper with your heel lightly touching the wall.
  3. Mark the longest part of your foot (tip of your big toe) on the paper with a pencil.
  4. Measure the distance from the edge of the paper to the mark in centimeters (CM).
  5. Match your measurement in CM against the size chart table above to find your ideal EU size!
