FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about shopping thrifted shoes with Hidden_Rack

Are all shoes on Hidden_Rack 100% authentic?
Yes! Absolutely 100%. We have zero tolerance for counterfeit or replica footwear. Every single pair is hand-inspected by our team for serial code alignment, stitching precision, sole density, and material quality before being listed.
How does the condition rating system work?
We use a strict 1-to-10 grading scale:
• 9.5/10 (Mint / Like New): Worn 1-2 times, zero star loss on soles, looks crisp.
• 9.0/10 (Near Mint): Very clean upper, minor midsole creasing, sturdy sole.
• 8.5/10 (Great Condition): Normal light wear, deep cleaned, 100% wearable with plenty of life left.
Are the shoes cleaned and disinfected before delivery?
Yes. Every sneaker undergoes a thorough deep cleaning process using premium shoe care products. Insoles and interior uppers are sanitized with UV antibacterial treatment to guarantee 100% hygiene upon arrival.
What payment methods do you accept in Pakistan?
We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) nationwide across Pakistan. We also accept Direct Bank Transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and major Credit/Debit cards.
How long will my order take to arrive?
Deliveries to major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi) typically take 2 to 4 business days. Deliveries to other cities across Punjab, Sindh, KPK, and Balochistan take 3 to 5 business days.
Can I exchange or return a pair if the size doesn't fit?
Yes! Because thrift shoes are typically 1-of-1 in size, we offer a 7-Day Easy Exchange Policy. If the shoe doesn't fit, you can exchange it for store credit or another available pair from our drop. Please check our Returns & Exchange Policy for details.
