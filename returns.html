RETURNS & EXCHANGE POLICY

Fair and transparent 7-Day Exchange policy for thrift footwear buyers in Pakistan

1. 7-DAY EXCHANGE GUARANTEE

Because every pair on Hidden_Rack is a unique 1-of-1 vintage thrift item, sizes are limited to what is currently in stock. If your received footwear does not fit as expected or if there is an unlisted defect, you are eligible for an exchange within 7 days of delivery.

2. EXCHANGE CONDITIONS

To be eligible for an exchange:

  • The item must be in the exact condition in which it was delivered.
  • The shoe must not have been worn outdoors or altered in any way.
  • You must initiate the exchange request within 7 calendar days of receipt.

3. HOW TO INITIATE AN EXCHANGE

1. Contact our Customer Support team on WhatsApp at +92 300 1234567 or email support@hiddenrack.pk with your Order ID and photos of the item.
2. Our team will verify your request and assist you in selecting a replacement pair from our available drop, or issuing a Store Credit coupon.
3. Ship the item back via courier (Leopard/TCS/M&P) to our warehouse address in Islamabad.

4. REFUNDS & STORE CREDIT

Store credit is issued for all approved exchanges. Direct cash refunds are processed if the received item was significantly different from its listed condition grade or photos.
